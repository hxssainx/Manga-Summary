Let's talk about them one by one.

Umi no Misaki

Umi no Misaki is a romance manga centered around Nagi, a young man who moves to a remote island and becomes involved with three mysterious girls. The story focuses on romance, relationships, jealousy, and the island's traditions. As Nagi spends more time on the island, he becomes connected to its mysterious culture and the lives of the girls. The manga combines romance with supernatural elements and explores complicated relationships and emotions.

Chainsaw Man

Chainsaw Man follows Denji, a poor young man who spends his life hunting devils to pay off his debts. His only companion is Pochita, a small chainsaw devil. After a tragic event, Denji gains the ability to transform into Chainsaw Man and joins the Public Safety Devil Hunters. The story is filled with action, horror, comedy, and emotional moments. It also explores loneliness, friendship, desire, manipulation, and Denji's wish to live a normal life.

Sakamoto Days

Sakamoto Days follows Taro Sakamoto, a legendary hitman who leaves his dangerous career behind after falling in love and starting a family. He now runs a small convenience store and wants to live a peaceful life. However, his past catches up with him when dangerous assassins begin appearing. Despite becoming a family man, Sakamoto still possesses incredible fighting abilities. The manga combines action, comedy, friendship, and exciting battles.

Beatrice

Beatrice is a fantasy romance manhwa centered around Claris, a princess whose life changes after her kingdom falls. Forced to hide her identity, she becomes a servant and struggles to survive in a completely different world. She eventually meets Duke Eustace, a powerful man with his own complicated past. Their relationship develops slowly as they learn more about each other. The story combines romance, fantasy, political conflict, social inequality, and character development.

Mashle: Magic and Muscles

Mashle: Magic and Muscles takes place in a world where magic determines a person's social status. Mash Burnedead, however, was born without magical abilities. To protect his peaceful life with his father, Mash enters a prestigious magic school and attempts to become a Divine Visionary. Instead of using magic, Mash relies on his incredible physical strength. The manga combines comedy, action, magic, and school life.

Naruto

Naruto follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who dreams of becoming Hokage. Because the Nine-Tailed Fox is sealed inside him, Naruto grows up feeling isolated and rejected. Despite this, he refuses to give up on his dream. Alongside Sasuke, Sakura, and their teacher Kakashi, Naruto goes on dangerous missions and becomes stronger. The story explores friendship, rivalry, loneliness, sacrifice, and the consequences of hatred.

One Piece

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy, a cheerful pirate who dreams of finding the legendary One Piece and becoming the Pirate King. After gaining rubber-like abilities from a Devil Fruit, Luffy begins his journey across the sea and forms the Straw Hat Pirates. Together, they explore strange islands, meet countless characters, and face pirates, Marines, and powerful enemies. The series combines comedy, action, adventure, friendship, and emotional storytelling.

A Couple of Cuckoos

A Couple of Cuckoos is a romantic comedy about Nagi Umino and Erika Amano. They discover that they were accidentally switched at birth, which causes their families to become connected. Their parents eventually arrange for them to marry, creating an unusual situation. Nagi already has feelings for someone else, while Erika has her own reasons for accepting the arrangement. The manga combines romance, comedy, school life, family relationships, and misunderstandings.

We Never Learn

We Never Learn follows Nariyuki Yuiga, a hardworking student who wants to receive a scholarship to help his family. He is assigned to tutor three talented girls: Rizu Ogata, Fumino Furuhashi, and Uruka Takemoto. Although each girl is talented in one subject, she struggles with another. Nariyuki helps them prepare for their exams while becoming closer to them. The manga combines comedy, romance, school life, and personal growth.

The Player Who Can't Level Up

The Player Who Can't Level Up is a fantasy action manhwa about Kim Gigyu, a young man who awakens as a player but discovers that he cannot level up normally. Instead of giving up, Gigyu discovers that his abilities work differently from those of other players. He begins exploring dangerous gates and fighting monsters while discovering the hidden potential of his powers. The story features dungeon battles, monsters, powerful enemies, mysterious organizations, and secrets about the world.

Naruto: Shippuden

Naruto: Shippuden represents the second major part of Naruto's story, following Naruto after he returns to the Hidden Leaf Village following years of training. Naruto continues his goal of becoming Hokage while trying to bring Sasuke back to the village. The story becomes darker as Naruto and his friends face the Akatsuki and other powerful enemies. It explores themes of war, revenge, friendship, sacrifice, loss, and the cycle of hatred.

Nisekoi: False Love

Nisekoi: False Love follows Raku Ichijo, the son of a yakuza leader, who becomes involved with Chitoge Kirisaki, the daughter of a rival gang leader. To prevent conflict between their families, they are forced to pretend to be a couple even though they constantly argue. Raku also has feelings for another girl and remembers a childhood promise connected to a mysterious locket. The manga combines romance, comedy, school life, friendship, and mystery.

Fire Punch

Fire Punch is a dark fantasy manga set in a frozen world where humanity is struggling to survive. The story follows Agni, a young man with extraordinary regenerative abilities, whose life is changed forever after a devastating event. Driven by revenge, Agni begins a brutal journey through a world filled with violence, suffering, and strange supernatural powers. The manga explores themes of revenge, morality, survival, identity, and the consequences of hatred. It combines dark fantasy, action, tragedy, and psychological elements to create a unique and unpredictable story.

I Want to Eat Your Pancreas

I Want to Eat Your Pancreas follows a quiet high school boy who discovers the diary of his classmate Sakura Yamauchi. He learns that Sakura has a terminal illness and has limited time left. Although they have completely different personalities, they slowly become friends. Sakura is energetic and wants to enjoy every moment, while the protagonist is quiet and prefers to stay away from others. Their friendship changes both of them as they spend time together.

To Your Eternity

To Your Eternity follows Fushi, an immortal being who is sent to Earth by an unknown creator. At first, Fushi has no human identity and gradually learns about the world by taking the forms of beings he encounters. Through the people he meets, he experiences friendship, love, loss, pain, and happiness. Every relationship changes Fushi and helps him understand humanity. As he grows stronger, he faces mysterious creatures called Nokkers and increasingly dangerous challenges.
